# Stay Safe Online – UPI Safety Checklist

-Never share your OTP or UPI PIN with anyone
-Do not approve any unknown payment request
-Verify the sender before making any transactions
-Do not click on suspicious links or messages
-Use official apps for banking transactions only
-Use two-factor authentication when possible
-Check your bank statements regularly
-Do not answer calls claiming to be from bank officials
-Do not scan unknown QR codes for receiving money
-Report fraud immediately on cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930
