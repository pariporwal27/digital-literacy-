# Case Study - UPI Fraud
UPI fraud is one of the most common forms of cybercrime in India, wherein a person is tricked into making a fraudulent transaction. Such types of fraud are common against students who are not aware of digital payment systems. In a real-life scenario of a UPI fraud case from 2023, a student was selling a second-hand product online.

The incident occurred when the student posted the product online and was immediately approached by a person who claimed to be a genuine buyer. After a few hours of posting the ad online, the fraudster expressed interest in purchasing the product and asked the student to share their UPI ID to make the payment. However, the fraudster sent a payment request to the student and simultaneously called the student to assist them with the process. The student was made to believe that it was necessary to enter the UPI PIN to receive the payment. The student, trusting the caller, entered the PIN, and in a matter of minutes, Rs. 5,000 was deducted from the account. Immediately after the transaction, the fraudster went out of reach and blocked all communication.

The case illustrates the way scammers trick the victims to authorize the transactions themselves, leading to instant financial loss. Such cases have been reported on the National Cyber Crime Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) in India.
